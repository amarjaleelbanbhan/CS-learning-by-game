## Character Profiles

### Security Chief Reyes

- **Department**: Security District
- **Personality**: Pragmatic, strict, deeply respectful of deterministic systems. He believes that non-determinism is a liability.
- **Lore**: Spent twelve cycles defending the Outer Rim servers before retiring to the Academy to train cadets in firewalls and perimeter DFA construction.

### ORBIT (Laboratory Assistant)

- **Department**: Player Personal Laboratory
- **Personality**: Enthusiastic, slightly sarcastic, highly helpful.
- **Lore**: A refurbished assistant core saved from the scrap heap of the Compiler Research Wing. He has calibrated over 10,000 builder canvases.

## Academy Lore & History

### The Great Automata Convergence

In the year 88, the first non-deterministic compiler achieved parallel branching capability without hardware degradation. This event, known as the **Convergence**, allowed researchers to explore infinite search spaces simultaneously, spawning the Academy's Quantum Research Labs.

### The Turing Boundary

A physical and digital memorial erected in the central archive. It commemorates the discovery of the Halting Problem, reminding engineers of the mathematical boundaries of computation.

## Department Guidelines

### 1. Security District

- **Theme**: Blue high-contrast mechanical steel.
- **Focus**: Clean DFAs, deterministic boundaries, zero edge-case leaks.

### 2. Quantum Research Lab

- **Theme**: Violet glow, parallel branch visualizers.
- **Focus**: NFAs, non-deterministic transitions, parallel exploration.

### 3. Grammar Tower

- **Theme**: Warm golden scroll motifs and compiler grids.
- **Focus**: Context-Free Grammars, derivations, lexical parses.
