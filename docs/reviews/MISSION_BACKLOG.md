## 2. Advanced Proofs & Undecidability

### The Halting Duel (toa.design.halting-reduction-01) [PLANNED]

- **Topic**: Halting Problem & Reductions.
- **Objective**: Map the input of one mystery machine into another to show that if decidability holds, contradiction arises.

### Myhill-Nerode Minimizer (toa.design.myhill-nerode-equivalence-01) [PLANNED]

- **Topic**: Equivalence classes and Myhill-Nerode theorem.
- **Objective**: Group infinitely many strings into their respective equivalence classes under a relation $\equiv_L$ to discover the minimal number of states for a DFA.
