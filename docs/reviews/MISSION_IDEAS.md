# Project ARC Reactor — Mission & Puzzle Ideas

Detailed proposals for future missions, interactive puzzles, and capstone boss gates.

---

## Boss Gate: The Minimization Core

- **District**: Security District (Capstone)
- **Goal**: Optimize a giant, bloated DFA with 24 states down to its minimal form.
- **Puzzle Mechanic**:
  - The player uses a transition table and clicks pairs of states to mark them as "distinguishable".
  - A scanner sweep runs across the grid, highlighting equivalence classes.
  - The player then merges identical classes in real time to rebuild the minimal firewall core.
- **Atmosphere**: Deep red emergency alarms and high-intensity neon sweeps.
- **Credits Reward**: 200 EC.

---

## Weekly Challenge: The Parity Engine

- **Goal**: Build a machine that recognizes binary strings where the number of occurrences of "01" matches the number of occurrences of "10".
- **Difficulty**: Hard (5/5).
- **Hints**:
  - Compare this to a simpler language: strings starting and ending with the same character.
  - Track states for tracking the last seen character.
- **Credits Reward**: 500 EC.

## Mission: The Kleene Intersect

- **District**: Quantum Research Lab
- **Concept**: NFA Intersection (product construction).
- **Objective**: Solve a dual-graph puzzle where two separate NFA pathways run on the same tape. Find the set of states where both pathways simultaneously reach their accepting conditions.
- **XP Reward**: 350 XP.
