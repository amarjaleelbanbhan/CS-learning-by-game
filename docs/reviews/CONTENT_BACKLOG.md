## Designed Missions (V2/Future Pipeline)

### 1. The Pumping Lemma Gauntlet

- **ID**: `toa.design.pumping-lemma-01`
- **District**: `pumping-dungeon`
- **Concept**: Non-regularity proofs using the Pumping Lemma.
- **Objective**: Play an interactive game against the AI mentor. Choose a language, let the AI choose the pumping length $p$, select a string $w \in L$ with $|w| \ge p$, and show that no matter how the AI splits $w = xyz$, there is some $i$ where $xy^iz \notin L$.

### 2. Context-Free Ambiguity Scanner

- **ID**: `toa.design.cfg-ambiguity-01`
- **District**: `grammar-tower`
- **Concept**: Grammatical ambiguity, leftmost/rightmost derivations.
- **Objective**: Find a string in a given Context-Free Grammar (CFG) that has at least two distinct parse trees, proving the grammar is ambiguous.
